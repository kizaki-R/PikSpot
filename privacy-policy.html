PikSpot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-02

PikSpot is an Android application that helps users capture map coordinates from supported links and manage them locally on their device.

1. Information We Process

PikSpot may process the following information:

  • Map links or shared text that contain coordinates.
  • Coordinates and notes saved by the user inside the app.
  • Clipboard content when the app copies captured coordinates for the user.
  • Advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob and its partners.

2. How PikSpot Uses Information

  • To extract coordinates from supported incoming links or shared text.
  • To store coordinate history, notes, pinned items, and export data such as GPX files.
  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob.

3. Local Storage

Coordinate history, notes, and related app data are stored locally on the user's device unless otherwise stated. PikSpot does not provide account registration or cloud sync at this time.

4. Advertising

PikSpot uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process certain data, which can include:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as advertising IDs.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address.
  • App interactions related to ad delivery and measurement.
  • Diagnostics and performance information.

For more information about how Google uses data in advertising services, please visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Data Sharing

PikSpot may share data with Google AdMob and related service providers solely for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance purposes.

6. Data Security

Data transmitted by the app and integrated services is intended to be protected using standard security measures such as encrypted network connections where applicable.

7. Children's Privacy

PikSpot is not designed specifically for children. The app is intended for general utility use and is not directed to users under the age requirements defined by applicable platform policies.

8. Your Choices

  • You may stop using PikSpot at any time.
  • You may clear locally stored coordinate data from within the app.
  • You may manage ad-related preferences through your device or Google settings where available.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page by changing the "Last updated" date above.
